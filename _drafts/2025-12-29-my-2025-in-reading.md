# Introduction
Fortunately as we come ot the end of this year, I'm in much better health than in 2024, avoiding whatever superflus
that have been circulating around the UK and the US, but I thought, [similar to last year](_posts/2024-06-19-what-I-would-tell-myself-phd.md) I'd do a little bit of reflecting on the books I'd read this year - what hit the mark, what didn't, and what I want to more and less of in 2025.
Similar to last year, I've read a bit across fiction, non-fiction, with most of the books this year being winners.
According to my [story graph account](https://app.thestorygraph.com/profile/conor_hamill), I've managed to hit my goal of 15 books this year, which exludes some of the technical and career-focussed books I read; I've decided to keep the books on story graph those I read "for pleasure", rather than for work, althought I'm lucky enough that those two often overlap.
